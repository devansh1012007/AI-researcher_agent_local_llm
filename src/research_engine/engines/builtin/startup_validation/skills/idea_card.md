# Skill: Reading the idea card
The question is an idea card. Line 1 is the idea. Later lines are `key: value` fields:
problem, customer_segment, alternative, price_point, riskiest_assumption, geography,
existing_traction, founder_constraints.
- Keep the user's words. Put each given field into scope or constraints; never invent one.
- A missing customer_segment or problem is an ambiguity to record, not something to guess.
- Treat existing_traction claims as unverified user statements, not evidence.
