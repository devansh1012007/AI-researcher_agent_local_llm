# Skill: Moat Analysis & "Why Hasn't This Been Built?"

## Objective
Stress-test every candidate startup opportunity against structural barriers, incumbent bundling risk, and historical failure modes before recommending action.

## Mandatory questions
1. **Why hasn't this been built yet?** Evaluate whether the barrier was technical infeasibility, adverse unit economics, distribution lock-in, regulatory friction, or lack of real buyer urgency.
2. **Counter-evidence pair:** Present the strongest evidence-backed argument FOR and the strongest evidence-backed argument AGAINST each opportunity side by side.
3. **Defensibility & moat:** Assess switching costs, proprietary workflow data, network effects, or regulatory licensing vs. thin LLM wrapper risk.
