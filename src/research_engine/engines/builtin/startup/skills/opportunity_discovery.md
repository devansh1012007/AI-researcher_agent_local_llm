# Skill: Opportunity Discovery Playbook

## Objective
Identify evidence-backed startup opportunities from recurring customer complaints, manual workarounds, regulatory catalysts, and underserved segments.

## Discovery patterns
1. Repeated high-severity workflow complaints across independent forums or reviews.
2. Spreadsheet/manual glue workarounds where buyers already spend time or money.
3. Regulatory or compliance mandates forcing process changes on SMBs or enterprises.
4. Incumbent pricing or complexity lock-out leaving a specific sub-segment underserved.

## Hard rules
- Never invent an opportunity without citing supporting pain or workaround evidence.
- Every candidate opportunity must also search for counter-evidence (failed startups, churn complaints, or free bundled alternatives).
