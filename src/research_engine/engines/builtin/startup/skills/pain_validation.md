# Skill: Pain & Demand Validation Hierarchy

## Objective
Classify customer pain and demand signals strictly along the behavioral validation hierarchy (`VALIDATION_EVIDENCE_HIERARCHY`).

## Validation ladder (weakest to strongest)
1. `casual_opinion` (0.1): Unprompted forum commentary without active workaround.
2. `survey_intention` (0.3): Stated hypothetical willingness to try or pay.
3. `interview_evidence` (0.5): Detailed first-hand account of past workflow failure.
4. `prototype_usage` (0.7): Active trial or pilot adoption.
5. `repeated_behavioral_usage` (0.9): Sustained retention or heavy manual workaround investment.
6. `payment` (1.0): Actual customer spending, invoices, or paid contracts.

## Hard rule
Vendor venture capital fundraising or company valuation is NEVER customer payment evidence.
