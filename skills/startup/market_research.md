# Skill: Market Research (startup mode)

## Objective
Build an evidence-grounded picture of a market: structure, segments, pains,
alternatives, competitors, pricing, signals.

## Source preferences
Government/statistics (tier 1) → filings and industry reports (tier 2) → credible press
(tier 3) → community/forum signals (tier 4, observations only).

## Evidence requirements
- Market numbers: metric + value + unit + currency + period + who published it.
- Customer pain: verbatim complaints/interviews/survey data with source + date.
- Competitors: name, positioning, pricing page evidence, funding if reported.
- "why now": technology/regulatory/behavior shifts each tied to dated sources.

## Hard separation (non-negotiable)
`observed evidence` ≠ `inference` ≠ `assumption`.
"People complain about X" must never become "there is a billion-dollar market for X" —
the latter requires sizing evidence from tier 1-2 sources. The engine stores claims with
kind FACT/INFERENCE/ASSUMPTION and reports keep the labels visible.

## Failure conditions
- All market-size figures trace to one organization → record single-source risk gap.
- Forum enthusiasm without spending/adoption signals → mark as unvalidated demand.
