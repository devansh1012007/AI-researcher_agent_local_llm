# Skill: Customer Pain

## Objective
Document who hurts, about what, how intensely, and what they currently do about it.

## Evidence standards
- Verbatim quotes from customers/users (forums, reviews, interviews, surveys) with URLs.
- Frequency/intensity signals (upvotes, repeated complaints) noted as metadata.
- Distinguish stated pain from inferred pain; the latter is INFERENCE kind.

## Reasoning rules
- A pain without an existing workaround may be a non-problem; record current alternatives.
- Distinguish "annoyance" from "spending money to avoid".
- Segment pains by customer type; do not average across segments.

## Output
Pain items linked to segment, evidence IDs, and severity rationale.
